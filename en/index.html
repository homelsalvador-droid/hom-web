FAQ

What owners ask us.

What do I have to do as an owner? +

Nothing. We handle the full management. During the month we pay your property’s day-to-day costs (supplies, minor repairs and whatever it takes to operate). At the end of the month we send you a report reconciling those costs and charge you once. That is the only contact you need to have with us.

Can I still use my property on some weekends? +

Yes. Let us know in advance and we block those dates on the calendar.

Who pays for cleaning? +

Cleaning is charged to the guest on each booking as a cleaning fee. We coordinate it and check that the property is ready.

How and when do I get paid? +

Airbnb deposits directly into your bank account. For transparency, we never receive or handle your money. To save on transfer fees, we recommend setting a minimum payout threshold in your account instead of a transfer for every booking.

I live abroad. How does it work? +

Many of our owners live in the United States. Everything is handled over WhatsApp and video call, and you get the monthly report wherever you are.
